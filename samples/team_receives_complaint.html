Under the hood (actual code paths):
• portal_submit_complaint() inserts the ticket and calls ping_team() → writes a row into notifications for every active technician/admin scoped to that customer (tenant admins get it too, via their care list).
• The client polls /api/notifications/ping; when it sees a new unread id it flips the red bell dot and calls playAlertSound() — the per-user custom sound you added (or a synthesized chime).
• accept_complaint() stores accepted_by/accept_reply, auto-assigns the acceptor, and fires one ping_followers() notification so the whole team knows who accepted.
